Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026. This is a translation. If the two versions differ, the German version applies.

In short: Plantyman stores everything you enter only on your device. There is no account, no app server, no advertising and no analytics or tracking services. I never get to see your tasks.

2. Data in the app

Your tasks, categories, notes, due dates, points, streaks, the state of your plant and your settings, including your language choice, are stored only in the app's storage on your device. They are not transferred to me or to third parties. The app does not connect to a server for any of its features.

If you uninstall the app or clear its data in the Android settings, this data is removed. I cannot restore it because I don't have a copy.

3. Notifications

If you switch on the daily reminder or use the timer, the app asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled and shown on your device. They may contain the name of a task. No data is sent to a server for this. You can revoke the permission at any time in the Android settings.

4. Backup

With “Create backup” you make a copy of your data as text or a file. What happens to it is up to you, for example whether you save it in another app or send it somewhere. Passing it on is subject to the privacy terms of the service you choose for that.

5. Feedback by email

“Send feedback” opens your email app with a prepared message to me. Only when you send it do I receive your email address and the content of your message. I use both only to read your feedback and to reply if needed. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in answering your request and improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). I delete the message once it has been dealt with and no retention obligation applies.

My mailbox is run by Google (Gmail). Google processes the message as the email provider. Details are in Google's privacy policy.

6. Download via Google Play

When you install Plantyman via Google Play, Google processes data such as your Google account and information about your device. Google is responsible for this itself, and the Google Play terms apply. Google provides me as the developer with aggregated statistics, such as the number of installs and uninstalls and technical crash reports. I cannot identify individual people from them.

7. This website

These pages are provided via GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit, GitHub processes technically necessary data, in particular your IP address, to deliver the page and protect it from abuse. Data may be transferred to the USA. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in providing these pages securely and reliably (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Details are in GitHub's privacy statement.

This website sets no cookies, loads no external fonts and uses no analytics or tracking services.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access the data I process about you, and to rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability (Art. 15 to 20 GDPR). You can object at any time to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Art. 21 GDPR). Just send me an email.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one responsible for me: the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia (Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen).

9. Changes

If the app changes so that other data is processed, I will update this policy. The current version is always on this page.
